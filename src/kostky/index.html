Farkle Rules

Roll 6 dice. Set aside scoring dice, then roll again or bank your points.

Scoring

  • Single 1 = 100 pts
  • Single 5 = 50 pts
  • Three of a kind = face x 100 (three 1s = 1000)
  • Four of a kind = face x 200
  • Five of a kind = face x 400
  • Six of a kind = face x 800
  • Straight (1-2-3-4-5-6) = 1500
  • Three pairs = 750

Gameplay

  • You must keep at least one scoring die each roll
  • Bank to save your turn points to your total
  • Roll again with remaining dice to push for more
  • Farkle — if no dice score, you lose all turn points
  • Hot Dice — if all 6 dice score, roll all 6 again
  • First to 10,000 points wins!
